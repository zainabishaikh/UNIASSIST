=== SECTION: POL-ELIG-01 | DEPT: ELIGIBILITY | ROLE: applicant | TITLE: B.Tech Undergraduate Engineering Eligibility Criteria ===
Program: Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech - CSE, AI & ML, Data Science, ECE, Mechanical)
Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
Academic Qualification:
- Candidates must have passed 10+2 / PUC / Equivalent examination from a recognized board with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry / Biotechnology / Biology / Computer Science / Electronics.
- Minimum Percentage & Aggregate Marks: 45% minimum aggregate percentage in Physics, Mathematics, and the third optional subject for General Merit B.Tech CSE admission (40% aggregate for SC, ST, and Category-1 candidates of Karnataka).
Accepted Entrance Examinations:
- KCET (Karnataka Common Entrance Test), COMED-K UGET, JEE Main, or DSAT (Dayananda Sagar Admission Test).
Lateral Entry (2nd Year / 3rd Semester):
- Candidates with a 3-year Engineering Diploma with at least 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST/Cat-1) and valid DCET rank are eligible for lateral entry B.Tech admission.

=== SECTION: POL-ELIG-02 | DEPT: ELIGIBILITY | ROLE: applicant | TITLE: MCA Eligibility Criteria, Admission & Mathematics Bridge Rules ===
Program: Master of Computer Applications (MCA)
Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
MCA Admission & Academic Qualification:
- Candidates seeking MCA admission must have passed a recognized Bachelor's Degree of minimum 3 years duration in BCA, B.Sc. (Computer Science / IT), B.E. / B.Tech in CSE/IT, or an equivalent degree.
- Alternatively, graduates in B.Sc., B.Com., or B.A. are eligible for MCA admission provided they studied Mathematics at the 10+2 (Class 12) level OR at the Graduation level.
- Minimum Aggregate Marks: At least 50% aggregate marks in the qualifying degree examination across all years/semesters for General Merit candidates (45% aggregate for SC, ST, and Category-1 candidates of Karnataka).
Mathematics Bridge Course Provision:
- Non-Mathematics graduates who studied Mathematics in Class 12 are directly eligible. Candidates who have neither Class 12 Mathematics nor Graduation Mathematics must complete a mandatory University Bridge Course in Discrete & Foundation Mathematics during Semester 1, subject to Dean's approval.
Accepted Entrance Examinations for MCA:
- Valid score/rank in Karnataka PGCET, KMAT, or DSAT (Dayananda Sagar Admission Test).

=== SECTION: POL-ELIG-03 | DEPT: ELIGIBILITY | ROLE: applicant | TITLE: MBA, BCA & B.Sc. Program Eligibility Norms ===
1. Master of Business Administration (MBA - 2 Years):
- Recognized Bachelor's degree in any discipline (minimum 3 years) with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST/Cat-1 candidates).
- Accepted Entrance Exams: PGCET, KMAT, CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, or DSAT.
2. Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA - 3 Years / 4 Years Honours):
- Pass in 10+2 / PUC / Equivalent in any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts) with minimum 45% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST/Cat-1). Mathematics or Computer Science at 10+2 is preferred.
3. Bachelor of Science (B.Sc. Biotechnology / Microbiology / Data Science - 3 Years):
- Pass in 10+2 / PUC with Science stream (PCB or PCM) and minimum 45% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST/Cat-1).
- Selection is based on DSAT / 10+2 Merit Score.

=== SECTION: POL-EXAM-01 | DEPT: EXAMINATIONS | ROLE: student | TITLE: SEE Exam Schedule, Hall Ticket & Attendance Shortage Rules ===
Semester End Examination (SEE) & Attendance Regulations (Enrolled Students):
- SEE Exam Schedule Release: Official Controller of Examinations (CoE) timetable is published on the Student ERP Portal 21 days prior to the commencement of theory examinations (typically November for Odd Semester and April/May for Even Semester).
- Minimum Attendance Requirement:
  * 85% attendance per course is mandatory to appear in SEE without restriction.
  * 75% to 84% attendance may be condoned ONLY on verified medical hospitalization or university-represented sports/NCC grounds upon submitting supporting certificates + INR 1,500 condonation fee per course to the Dean.
  * Below 75% attendance results in automatic detention (NSAR - Not Satisfied Attendance Requirement) in that course; the student must re-register when offered next.
- Exam Hall Ticket Generation:
  * Hall tickets are unlocked on the ERP Portal 5 days before the exam ONLY after clearing 100% tuition dues, exam registration fee, and meeting the attendance threshold.

=== SECTION: POL-EXAM-02 | DEPT: EXAMINATIONS | ROLE: student | TITLE: CIE Internal Assessment, CGPA Grading & Passing Standards ===
Evaluation Pattern & Academic Progression Standards:
- Assessment Split: Every theory course is evaluated out of 100 marks comprising 50% Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE: 2 Mid-Term Tests, Quizzes, Assignments/Labs) and 50% Semester End Examination (SEE).
- Minimum Passing Criteria:
  * Minimum 40% marks in CIE (20/50) is required to become eligible for SEE.
  * Minimum 35% marks in SEE (18/50) and 40% overall aggregate (40/100) is required to pass a course.
- 10-Point Relative/Absolute CGPA Grading Scale:
  * O (90-100): 10 pts | A+ (80-89): 9 pts | A (70-79): 8 pts | B+ (60-69): 7 pts | B (55-59): 6 pts | C (50-54): 5 pts | P (40-49): 4 pts | F (<40): 0 pts.
- Revaluation & Makeup Exam: Students may apply for answer-script photocopy and revaluation within 5 working days of result declaration (INR 600 per paper for photocopy, INR 1,000 for revaluation).
